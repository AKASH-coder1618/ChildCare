Our Facilities & Programs

Comprehensive care and development programs designed for every child's wellbeing

🏠 Residential Facilities

Modern dormitories with comfortable beds, clean bathrooms, and recreational spaces. Each child has a safe, clean space they can call home with proper ventilation and lighting.

📚 Education Center

Well-equipped classrooms, library with 5000+ books, computer lab with internet, and qualified teachers. We provide tutoring support for academic excellence.

🏥 Medical Facility

In-house medical clinic, regular health checkups, vaccinations, and emergency care. We partner with local hospitals for specialized treatments.

🍽️ Dining Hall

Nutritious meals prepared by experienced cooks. Three balanced meals daily plus snacks. We consider dietary preferences and health conditions.

⚽ Recreation Area

Outdoor playground, sports equipment, games, and entertainment facilities. Indoor games room with chess, board games, and creative activities.

🎓 Skills Training

Vocational training programs including tailoring, carpentry, computers, and handicrafts. Job placement assistance for older children.

Our Special Programs

  • Scholarship Program: Full or partial scholarships for deserving students pursuing higher education
  • Mentorship Program: One-on-one mentoring with professionals and successful alumni
  • Counseling Services: Professional psychological support and trauma-informed care
  • Life Skills Training: Communication, financial literacy, health awareness, and independence skills
  • Cultural Activities: Dance, music, theater, and traditional art programs
  • Sports Development: Coaching in cricket, badminton, athletics, and other sports
  • Summer Programs: Educational camps, skill workshops, and recreational activities
  • Transition Program: Preparation for independent living for children aging out of the facility
